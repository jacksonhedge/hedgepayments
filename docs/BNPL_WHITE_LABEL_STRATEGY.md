## 🚨 Risk Mitigation

### Risk 1: Low Adoption
**Mitigation:**
- Market HedgePay heavily to fraternities
- Offer first-time user promotions
- Show savings vs. credit card interest
- Target high-dues chapters ($1,000+)

### Risk 2: High Default Rates
**Mitigation:**
- Partner with provider who bears credit risk (Gratify, Certegy)
- Add parental co-signer for amounts >$500
- Start conservative, expand as data improves

### Risk 3: Regulatory Changes
**Mitigation:**
- Choose provider with compliance team
- Monitor state BNPL regulations
- Ensure provider handles licensing

### Risk 4: Provider Dependency
**Mitigation:**
- Negotiate multi-year contract with rate locks
- Plan for provider switch if needed
- Keep integration abstracted (easy to swap providers)

## 📱 User Experience Design

### Bankroll App - Payment Request Flow

**Before HedgePay:**
```
Payment Request: $100 from Alice
[Pay Now] [Decline]
```

**After HedgePay:**
```
Payment Request: $100 from Alice

Payment Options:
○ Pay Now - Full Amount ($100)
● Pay with HedgePay - 4 payments of $25

  Today: $25
  Week 2: $25
  Week 4: $25
  Week 6: $25

  0% interest if paid on time

[Continue with HedgePay] [Pay Full Amount]
```

### Approval Flow:
1. User selects "Pay with HedgePay"
2. Quick info form (if first-time):
   - DOB
   - Last 4 SSN
   - Income (optional)
3. Instant approval/decline (< 30 seconds)
4. If approved: Show payment schedule, confirm
5. If declined: Offer to add parent as co-signer OR pay full amount
