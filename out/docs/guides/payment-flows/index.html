Card Payment + Chargeback Protection

CoinFlow provides 100% chargeback protection through AI fraud detection, 3D Secure, and device fingerprinting.

Zero chargeback liability. CoinFlow absorbs 100% of chargebacks on approved transactions. Merchants receive funds regardless of cardholder disputes. This is a key differentiator vs. traditional payment processors and open banking providers like Trustly.

Full Architecture Overview

How Hedge, CoinFlow, and Plaid work together to provide complete payment infrastructure.

Trustly comparison: Like Trustly, Hedge enables direct bank-to-merchant payments. Unlike Trustly, Hedge adds CoinFlow's 100% chargeback guarantee, multi-rail settlement (USDC, USD, card), and Plaid-powered bank verification purpose-built for the US market.

Security & Compliance

Enterprise-grade security across the full payment stack.

PCI DSS Level 1

Highest level of card data security. CoinFlow handles all PCI scope — Hedge merchants never touch raw card numbers.

SOC 2 Type II

Independently audited security controls covering availability, confidentiality, and processing integrity.

100% Chargeback Protection

CoinFlow absorbs all chargebacks on approved transactions. AI fraud scoring, 3D Secure, device fingerprinting, and behavioral analysis.

KYC / AML Compliance

Identity verification, OFAC sanctions screening, FinCEN registration, and anti-money laundering monitoring — all handled by CoinFlow.

Data Encryption

TLS 1.3 for data in transit, AES-256 for data at rest, HSM for key management. GDPR and CCPA compliant.

Plaid Bank Verification

Instant bank account verification via Plaid. No micro-deposits required. Supports 12,000+ financial institutions in the US.
